Mastering Wordle Hard Mode: Rules & Survival Tips

If regular mode in Wordle Unlimited starts feeling too easy, turning on Hard Mode brings a whole new challenge. In standard play, you can guess throwaway words to test multiple consonants at once. In Hard Mode, you are required to use every revealed clue in all subsequent guesses.

Key Takeaways

  • Strict Rules: Green letters must stay in place; yellow letters must be reused in the next guess.
  • Trap Hazard: Finding 4 green letters early on words like _IGHT or _OUND can force you into a guessing game.
  • Balanced Starters: Open with words like ROATE or SLATE to check critical consonants early.

The Rules of Hard Mode

  • Green Tiles: Any letter revealed in green must remain in that exact spot for all remaining guesses.
  • Yellow Tiles: Any letter revealed in yellow must be included somewhere in your next guess.

If a guess does not follow these rules, the board will shake and prompt you to enter a valid guess.

The Rhyme Trap (How Streaks Die)

The most common way players lose streaks in Hard Mode is hitting a 4-green pattern too early on rhyming words. For example, if you guess LIGHT on turn 2 and get _IGHT:

Because you are locked into guessing _IGHT words, you must test candidates one at a time:

  • NIGHT, MIGHT, FIGHT, RIGHT, SIGHT, TIGHT, BIGHT (7+ candidates)

With only 4 guesses left, you are at the mercy of luck unless you eliminated some of those consonants earlier.

Survival Tactics

Versatile Openers

Pick words like ROATE, SLATE, or CRANE from our Best Starting Words Guide to check key consonants right away.

Spread Out Letters

Avoid rushing to lock in 4 greens on turn 2 if multiple rhyming possibilities exist.

How to Enable Hard Mode

Click the Settings icon (⚙️) in the top header and turn on the "Hard Mode" toggle. You can practice as many games as you want on Wordle Unlimited.
