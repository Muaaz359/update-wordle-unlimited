Over 99% of 5-letter English words contain at least one vowel (A, E, I, O, U, or Y). Because vowels form the core of almost every word structure, figuring out which vowels are present—and where they sit—is the fastest way to solve puzzles consistently.

Key Takeaways

  • E and A: The most common letters in Wordle, appearing in over 40% of all solutions.
  • ADIEU + STORY: Tests all 5 standard vowels plus Y in your first two guesses.
  • Double Vowels: If only one vowel is found, think of words with double E or double O.

The Two-Turn Vowel Test

If you want to quickly map out the vowels in the secret word, try this two-step opener:

  • Guess 1: ADIEU (checks A, I, E, U)
  • Guess 2: STORY (checks O, Y plus S, T, R)

By turn 2, you will know exactly which vowels exist in the target word, whether any are in the right position, and whether the word uses Y as its vowel sound.

Watch Out for Double Vowels

About 15% of 5-letter target words have repeating vowels. If your initial guesses reveal only one vowel and nothing seems to fit, try words with:

  • Double E: BLEED, CHEEK, FLEET, GEESE, SWEET
  • Double O: BLOOD, BROOK, FLOOD, ROOST, SPOON
  • Vowel Pairs: BEACH, COACH, GRAIN, HOUSE, TRAIN
