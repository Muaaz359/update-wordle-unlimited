Knowing which letters appear most often—and which spots they typically land in—can give your Wordle game a big boost. Here is the letter frequency breakdown across the official 2,300+ target word list.

Key Takeaways

  • Top 12 letters: Make up more than 75% of all letter occurrences in 5-letter target words.
  • Spot 1: 'S' starts over 15% of all valid solutions.
  • Spot 5: 'E' and 'Y' finish over 35% of all solutions.

Where Letters Appear in 5-Letter Words

  • Position 1 (First Letter): S is the most common first letter (over 360 words), followed by C, B, T, P, A, and F. Starting with S words (like SLATE) lands first-letter green hits most frequently.
  • Position 2: A, O, E, I, U, and R. Over 85% of target words place a vowel in spot 2.
  • Position 3: A, I, O, E, U, and R. Common vowel digraphs like EA or OU sit here. Check our Vowel Guide.
  • Position 4: E, N, S, A, L, and T.
  • Position 5 (Last Letter): E and Y dominate spot 5, followed by T, R, L, and D.

Handling Rhyme Traps in Hard Mode

In Hard Mode, landing 4 green letters early can be dangerous if there are more candidate consonants than remaining guesses:

  • _IGHT: LIGHT, NIGHT, MIGHT, FIGHT, RIGHT, SIGHT, TIGHT (7+ words)
  • _ATCH: CATCH, MATCH, HATCH, PATCH, WATCH, LATCH (6+ words)
  • _OUND: BOUND, FOUND, HOUND, MOUND, POUND, ROUND, SOUND (7+ words)

In regular mode, if you spot an _IGHT pattern on turn 2, use turn 3 to test multiple starting consonants at once (e.g. guessing FLAMP to check F, L, M, P) before committing to your final answers!
