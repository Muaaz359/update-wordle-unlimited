How to Play Wordle Unlimited

Updated: Aug 2026 4 min read

Whether you are new to word games or just want to play without waiting 24 hours between puzzles, here is everything you need to know to get started on Wordle Unlimited.

Key Takeaways

  • Goal: Guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 tries.
  • Colors: 🟩 Green = Right spot, 🟨 Yellow = Wrong spot, ⬛ Gray = Not in word.
  • Infinite Play: Hit "Next Game" for an instant new puzzle anytime.

Game Rules

  • You have 6 attempts to guess a hidden 5-letter word.
  • Each guess must be a valid English word.
  • Press ENTER to submit your guess.

Tile Colors Explained

🟩 Green Tile

The letter is in the word and in the exact right spot.

🟨 Yellow Tile

The letter is in the word, but belongs in a different spot.

⬛ Gray Tile

The letter does not appear anywhere in the word.

Quick 3-Step Strategy

  1. Start with vowels: Open with words like ADIEU or CANOE. See our Best Starting Words Guide.
  2. Follow with consonants: Use turn 2 to test frequent letters like S, T, R, N with words like STORY. Read our Vowel Strategy.
  3. Deduce and solve: Combine the yellow and green hints to find the solution. Check our Letter Frequency Cheat Sheet.

Creating Custom Challenges

Want to challenge a friend? Click the '+' icon in the header, type in any 5-letter word, and copy the link to share with them!
