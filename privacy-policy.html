Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Wordle Unlimited (https://wordle-unlimited.app), we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is stored and how our site operates.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

You can play Wordle Unlimited without creating an account. We do not ask for or store:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Payment information

2. Browser Local Storage

To save your game progress between visits, our game uses your browser's native localStorage. This includes:

  • Your total games played, wins, and current/max streak
  • Your guess distribution count
  • Your dark mode and Hard Mode preferences

This data is saved directly on your own device and is never sent to our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Server Logs

Like most websites, our hosting servers collect basic technical access logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) solely for site security, DDoS protection, and server health monitoring.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out via our Contact Us page.
