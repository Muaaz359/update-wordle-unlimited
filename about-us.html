About Wordle Unlimited

Wordle Unlimited was born out of a simple frustration: daily word puzzles are great fun, but only getting one word every 24 hours leaves you wanting more.

We wanted a clean, fast place where you could practice your opening words, test tricky letter combinations, and play round after round without waiting for the clock to reset.

What We Care About

We designed Wordle Unlimited with a few simple principles:

Fast & Lightweight

Instant tile flips and immediate restarts with zero loading lag.

Privacy First

We don't track you. Your stats live right in your browser's local storage.

Careful Word Lists

Built using the official 5-letter English word library for fair puzzles every time.

Works Everywhere

Smooth on mobile, tablet, and desktop with support for dark mode and physical keyboards.
